    Chef & owner Marco Bellini

    The chef

    He left a good kitchen to build his own

    Marco trained eleven years in Modena before moving across the water, sick of cooking other people's food. He spent the first eighteen months of Forno Ember building a hearth by hand — a two-tonne white-oak firebox he fires with scrap timber from a mill up the valley, and relights by hand every morning at seven.

    The kitchen runs on that fire and almost nothing else. No gas, one oven, four burners. It means the bread is genuinely sour, the pizza crust genuinely blistered, and Marco genuinely short-tempered about a cold oven.

    • 01

      The Ember Margherita

      Sixty seconds, 485°C, buffalo mozzarella flown in weekly.

    • 02

      Cacio e Pepe, Properly

      Pecorino romano, cold pasta water, more pepper than looks sensible.

    • 03

      48-Hour Sourdough

      One flour, one levain kept since the first week of trading.
